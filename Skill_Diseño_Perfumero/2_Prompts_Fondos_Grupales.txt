=========================================
 PROMPTS: FONDOS PARA GRUPOS DE PERFUMES (6 a 7)
=========================================
*Copia y pega estos prompts en herramientas de IA (Midjourney, DALL-E, Gemini) para obtener fondos perfectos donde agrupar perfumes.*

PROMPT 1: MÁRMOL HORIZONTAL ESCALONADO (Ideal para Lujo/Nicho)
A professional, minimalist product photography background designed for a GROUP of products. Wide, horizontal white marble steps with subtle grey veining. Soft, diffused studio lighting coming from a large window off-camera. Elegant 'old money' aesthetic, highly realistic, completely empty background ready for placing multiple items, no text.

PROMPT 2: CICLORAMA BEIGE LISO (Ideal para Diseñador/Gourmand)
A seamless, wide, soft beige and sand-colored studio sweep infinity curve background for group product photography. Even, soft, beautiful diffused lighting. Vast empty horizontal space in the lower half to comfortably accommodate a row of 7 luxury products. Elegant, minimalist, neutral colors, highly realistic, empty background, no text.

PROMPT 3: REPISA DE PIEDRA OSCURA DRAMÁTICA (Ideal para Perfumería Árabe / Oud)
A very wide, elegant dark slate or black charcoal stone shelf against a dark textured wall. Designed for group product photography. Elegant soft spotlighting spanning across the width of the shelf, creating an upscale moody atmosphere with subtle shadows. Empty horizontal space, wide enough to line up luxury perfume bottles. Highly realistic, 8k resolution, no text.

PROMPT 4: PODIO ANCHO DE MADERA Y LUZ NATURAL (Ideal para Frescos/Unisex)
A wide, smooth light oak wood counter bathed in beautiful morning sunlight filtering through an unseen window. Soft, realistic leaf shadows dappled on the wall behind. Minimalist Japandi aesthetic. Empty horizontal space for placing multiple cosmetic or perfume bottles. Bright, clean, neutral colors. Highly realistic.
